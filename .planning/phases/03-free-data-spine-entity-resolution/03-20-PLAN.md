Purpose: 03-VALIDATION § Manual-Only Verifications names three things CI structurally cannot do —

whether a 0.4-confidence RGV row is a real business, and whether the ten fixture pairs really are
or are not the same business. No test can make either call.

min · Overture bbox read ~10 s + the ingest write · resolve pass ~2 min. Call it **~20 minutes**
end to end.

Output: the runbook, the committed measurements, and any constant changes those numbers force.
