that reds *more* tests than predicted means two guards share one refusal — which is precisely how

the revert is provable by construction (`git diff --stat` is empty) and is verified from
`pg_constraint` / `pg_indexes` / `pg_trigger` / `pg_get_functiondef` /
`information_schema.role_table_grants`, never from the fact that a script ran.
